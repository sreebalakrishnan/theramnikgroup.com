Our businesses

Six divisions. One owner.

Each business keeps its own name, team and customers. What the group provides is capital, patience and a standard to operate to.

01 — Real Estate

Property & Development

Commercial and mixed-use assets, held for income first.

The division owns and manages a portfolio of commercial, industrial and mixed-use property. We buy assets we understand in locations we know, hold them through cycles, and develop selectively — only where we can deliver something the local market genuinely lacks.

Development is a means, not the business. We are landlords who occasionally build, rather than builders who occasionally hold.

Activities: acquisition · asset management · selective development · long-term leasing

02 — Hospitality

Hotels & Leisure

Owned and operated properties, run on service rather than scale.

We own the buildings and run the operations, which keeps standards and incentives in the same hands. The division favours characterful properties in locations with durable demand over commodity rooms competing on price.

Investment is continuous rather than cyclical: because we are not preparing these assets for sale, refurbishment happens when it is needed, not when a transaction requires it.

Activities: hotel ownership · operations · food & beverage · events

03 — Industry

Manufacturing

Specialist industrial businesses with real engineering depth.

These are companies making components and equipment that are difficult to substitute, for customers who qualify their suppliers slowly and then stay for decades. Most joined the group through succession — founders retiring who wanted the business and its workforce kept intact.

Our contribution is capital equipment, capacity expansion and the patience to let long qualification cycles run their course.

Activities: precision manufacturing · engineered components · contract production

04 — Distribution

Retail & Supply

Distribution built on logistics discipline and long supplier relationships.

The group's original business was trading, and distribution remains close to its core. The division moves goods reliably, manages working capital tightly and maintains supplier relationships that in some cases predate the current generation of management.

Margins here are earned through execution, not positioning — which suits an owner willing to fund the unglamorous investments that execution requires.

Activities: wholesale distribution · logistics · retail operations

05 — Capital

Investments

Minority positions and structured capital, alongside owners who stay.

Not every good business is for sale, and not every owner wants to leave. The investments division backs companies where we take a minority position, provide liquidity or growth capital, and leave control where it is.

We are comfortable being the smaller partner. We are not comfortable being a passive one — we expect a seat at the table and information rights that let us be useful.

Activities: minority equity · structured capital · succession funding

06 — Ventures

Technology & New Bets

A small allocation to what might matter in a decade.

A deliberately limited share of group capital goes to early-stage businesses, usually adjacent to sectors we already operate in — logistics software, building systems, industrial automation.

We size these so that being wrong is survivable and being right is meaningful. Where a venture proves out, the group's operating businesses become its first serious customer.

Activities: early-stage investment · incubation · operating partnerships
