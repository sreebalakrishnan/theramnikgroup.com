Governance

How decisions get made.

  1. The board sets capital, not operations

    Group leadership decides where capital goes and what standards apply. Pricing, hiring, product and customer decisions belong to the operating companies.

  2. One plan a year, reviewed quarterly

    Each business agrees an annual plan with the group and reports against it. Between reviews, management is left to manage.

  3. Investment cases are written down

    Every material commitment is argued on paper before it is approved, and revisited afterwards against what actually happened.

  4. Succession is planned early

    Ownership across three generations has only worked because the next set of leaders — in the family and outside it — was identified long before it was needed.
